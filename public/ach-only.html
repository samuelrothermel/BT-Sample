ℹ️ About ACH Payments

What is ACH? Automated Clearing House (ACH) is a network used for electronically moving money between bank accounts across the United States.

Processing Time: ACH payments typically take 3-5 business days to process.

Sandbox Mode: This uses Braintree's sandbox environment for testing. Real bank accounts will not be charged.

Payment Method Storage: ACH payments require vaulting (securely storing) the bank account before processing. Your bank account will be automatically saved for future use.

Test Data: In sandbox, use any 9-digit routing number (e.g., 021000021) and account number. These will be tokenized and securely vaulted.

Mandate: By submitting payment, you authorize the merchant to debit your bank account through Braintree's ACH processing.

Note: Your bank account will be securely saved for future payments (required for ACH processing).
