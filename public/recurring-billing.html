💡 About Recurring Billing

  • Payment Method Vaulting: Credit cards must be vaulted before being used in subscriptions
  • Plan Required: You need to create a plan in your Braintree Control Panel first
  • Supported Payment Methods: Credit cards, PayPal, and Venmo work with subscriptions
  • Billing Cycle: The subscription will bill according to your plan's billing frequency
  • First Billing: First charge occurs immediately or at the billing cycle start (plan-dependent)

Creating a Test Plan

  1. Log into your Braintree Control Panel
  2. Navigate to Settings → Plans
  3. Click "Create New Plan"
  4. Set price, billing frequency, and trial period (optional)
  5. Save and copy the Plan ID
