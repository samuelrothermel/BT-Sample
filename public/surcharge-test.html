ℹ️ About Surcharges

What are surcharges?

Surcharges allow merchants to pass credit card processing fees to customers. The surcharge amount is displayed separately from the base transaction amount.

Surcharge Levels:

  • Network Level: Applied at the card network level (Visa, Mastercard, etc.)
  • Brand Level: Applied at the card brand level (credit vs. debit)

Note: Surcharges are subject to legal restrictions in some jurisdictions. Always check local regulations.
