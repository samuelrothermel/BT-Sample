ℹ️ About Automatic Vaulting

What happens when you pay:

  • Your payment is processed immediately
  • Your payment method is automatically saved securely
  • Future payments can use the saved method without re-entry
  • All payment methods (cards, PayPal, Venmo, etc.) are vaulted

Benefits:

  • Faster checkout for return customers
  • Secure tokenized storage (no raw card data stored)
  • Works with subscriptions and recurring billing
